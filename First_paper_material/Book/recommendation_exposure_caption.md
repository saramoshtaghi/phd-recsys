Mean number of target-category items in the top-15 recommendations for original users: (a) Thriller books, (b) Shopping applications, and (c) movies released in the 1990s. NMF and SVD are shown in the top and bottom rows, respectively. Book injections extend to 500 users, mobile injections to 300 users, and movie injections to 50 users. Baseline denotes training without synthetic users.

The x-axis is categorical within each dataset; injection counts and original user populations differ between datasets. The shared y-axis counts items out of 15.
